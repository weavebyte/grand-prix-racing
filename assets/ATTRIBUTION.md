# Visual sources

- Driver portraits: Formula 1 official 2026 drivers page, https://www.formula1.com/en/drivers . Local portraits are source images, not AI-generated likenesses.
- Venue photos: official race pages for Monaco, Italy, Japan, Great Britain and Singapore. These are venue photographs; the game does not claim they were taken in 2026. Exact image/page URLs are in sources.json.
- Photographic sky: “Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky)” by Greg Zaal / Jarod Guest, Poly Haven, CC0. https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky
- Asphalt texture: “Asphalt 02”, Poly Haven, CC0. https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_02

F1 photographs retain their original rights; downloading for this local prototype does not grant redistribution rights. No external publication has been performed. All race geometry, UI and gloves are locally authored stylized models, not official scanned circuit or driver models.
